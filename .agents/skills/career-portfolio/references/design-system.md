# Portfolio Design System

All themes share the same semantic HTML, components, accessibility behavior, and print geometry. Themes change tokens and visual treatment, not factual structure.

## Editorial

Warm paper, dark ink, vermilion accent, serif display type, asymmetric editorial rules. Best for narrative-heavy product and leadership work.

## Minimal

Bright white, precise black grid, electric blue accent, restrained sans typography. Best for corporate, consulting, and executive audiences.

## Technical

Deep navy, cyan and amber signals, monospaced metadata, blueprint grid. Best for architecture- and systems-heavy portfolios.

Do not mix theme vocabularies in one deck. Avoid generic purple gradients, dashboard-card repetition, excessive pills, and decorative charts without evidence.
