# Content Density

## Limits

- One primary claim per slide
- One title of roughly 4–12 words
- Summary of one or two sentences
- Usually 3–5 bullets
- Usually one dominant diagram, screenshot, or metric group
- Avoid shrinking type to preserve content

## Layout Choice

- `lead`: title and thesis above full-width evidence; use for architecture and complex flows
- `split`: narrative beside supporting diagram or metrics; default for project and decision slides
- `evidence`: visual proof dominates; use when the screenshot or result is the argument

When content does not fit, remove detail, split only when both slides have independent goals, or move detail to a case study.
