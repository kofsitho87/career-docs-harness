# Evidence Design

Every visible metric, screenshot, or architecture claim should map to stable memory IDs.

## Evidence Hierarchy

1. Public operational screenshot or artifact
2. Public repository or documentation
3. Source-backed quantitative result
4. Source-backed qualitative scope or decision
5. Inferred explanation, clearly marked and excluded from public claims

## Screenshots

- Remove personal, customer, tenant, call, and authentication identifiers.
- Crop to the region that proves the claim.
- Keep labels and context needed to interpret the image.
- Add alt text and a short caption.
- Do not use decoration as evidence.

## Diagrams

Show boundaries and flows that explain a decision. Distinguish the candidate's work, team systems, external services, and later changes.
