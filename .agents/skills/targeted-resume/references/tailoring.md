# Resume Tailoring Guidance

## Relevance Matrix

Classify each important job requirement:

- `direct`: verified experience using the same capability
- `adjacent`: verified transferable experience; describe the bridge honestly
- `gap`: no usable evidence; do not imply coverage
- `irrelevant`: not worth resume space for this target

## Allowed Changes

- Put the most relevant verified identity in the Summary.
- Move relevant competency groups earlier.
- Reorder projects and bullets within truthful chronology.
- Replace synonyms with the employer's standard terminology when meaning is unchanged.
- Remove unrelated details to meet length constraints.

## Strategy File

`targets/<slug>/strategy.md` should contain:

- role and company
- top requirements
- direct evidence with claim IDs
- adjacent evidence and honest bridge
- gaps
- selected resume sections and projects
- keyword map
- excluded material and reason

## Review

Compare every changed factual phrase with both the master resume and memory. A tailored resume may be narrower, never more factually expansive.
