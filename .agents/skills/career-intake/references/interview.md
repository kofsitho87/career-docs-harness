# Career Interview Guidance

## Priority Order

1. Identity, public contact, language, location, and target roles
2. Company, official title, actual role, start/end dates
3. Representative projects and the problems they addressed
4. Personal ownership, decisions, implementation, and team boundary
5. Quantitative or observable results and their evidence
6. Technologies used in production and depth of use
7. Education, open source, talks, writing, and relevant activities
8. Tone, length, visual preferences, and excluded content

## Ask Useful Questions

Ask for one missing decision or fact, not a broad autobiography.

- Weak: "프로젝트를 설명해주세요."
- Better: "이 프로젝트에서 본인이 직접 결정하거나 구현한 범위와 팀이 맡은 범위를 나눠 설명해 주세요."
- Better: "300개 고객사 도입 수치는 어느 시점 기준이며 공개 가능한 근거가 있나요?"

Use a short batch only when the questions share one context. Do not ask for information already present in a source unless two sources conflict.

## Evidence Follow-up

For a result, ask:

- What changed?
- Compared with what baseline?
- Over what period?
- What was the user's contribution?
- Is the number public, restricted, or private?
- Which document, URL, repository, or screenshot supports it?

If no quantitative evidence exists, capture observable scope or operational responsibility without inventing a number.

## Completion Signals

- The user can be positioned in one or two role identities.
- Career dates and titles are usable or explicitly conflicted.
- Recent experience is more detailed than older experience.
- Representative projects have problem, action, contribution, result, and evidence.
- Preferences and public/private boundaries are recorded.
