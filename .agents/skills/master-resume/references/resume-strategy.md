# Master Resume Strategy

## Selection

The master resume should cover the candidate's role family, not every historical detail. Prefer evidence that demonstrates:

- repeated problem-solving pattern
- production ownership
- system or product decisions
- meaningful operating scale
- measurable or observable results
- collaboration and leadership appropriate to seniority

## Section Order

1. Header and public links
2. Professional Summary
3. Core Competencies
4. Experience in reverse chronology
5. Education
6. Relevant open source, writing, talks, or activities

## Bullet Shape

Use the shortest structure that preserves meaning:

```text
Context or problem → candidate action/decision → result or operating effect
```

Avoid forcing all three parts into every bullet. A project can establish context once, followed by focused action/result bullets.

## Evidence and Claim IDs

Use only public, verified claims in visible copy. Place trace comments next to high-impact statements:

```md
<!-- claims: claim-voice-ai-scale, claim-automation-impact -->
```

## Master Versus Portfolio

The resume proves relevance quickly. Detailed architecture, workflow diagrams, tradeoffs, and screenshots belong in case studies or the portfolio.
