# Career Review Checklist

## Memory

- Schema validation passes.
- Stable IDs are unique.
- Verified claims have source refs.
- Conflicts are explicit and resolution state is consistent.
- User edits were not overwritten.
- Changelog and state reflect material changes.

## Master Resume

- Summary establishes identity with evidence.
- Career order, titles, dates, metrics, and technologies match memory.
- Public claims are verified and traceable.
- Recent experience receives the most space.
- Personal actions and results are clear.
- Skills reflect real depth and appear in context.
- Standard ATS headings and readable Markdown are used.
- Contact and links are intentional and valid.

## Targeted Resume

- Every factual phrase remains within master resume and verified memory.
- Strategy separates direct, adjacent, and gap requirements.
- Keywords are natural and supported.
- Tailoring improves relevance without changing seniority or ownership.
- The master resume was not accidentally overwritten.

## Case Study

- Problem, constraints, decisions, implementation, ownership, results, and lessons are separated.
- Architecture and operational claims match evidence.
- Team boundaries and later changes are explicit.
- Images are public-safe and referenced correctly.

## Portfolio

- Each slide has one communication goal.
- Important claims and evidence have stable IDs.
- Slide count matches configured range without filler.
- No clipping, overflow, missing assets, unreadable contrast, or broken links.
- HTML, PDF, and deployment output describe the same facts.
